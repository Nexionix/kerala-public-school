About Kerala Public School

Kerala Public School, Dewas is a co-educational English Medium School affiliated to MP Board, conducting classes Nursery to Class 10th. Due importance is given to Hindi also, so that the students have a deep understanding in all the subjects.

The School is centrally located within the vicinity of the city. Senior Teachers have been working with the School for more than 20 years, with vast experience and dedication. They take extra efforts to provide value education to their students.

A core strength of the School is that the teachers pay close attention to each student.

Our motto and aim is to impart quality education to all sections of students.
